# Changelog

All notable changes to Flow are documented here.

## 1.0.1 - 2026-10-21

- Updated Astro to 7.4.1 and other dependencies.
- Updated `AGENTS.md` for Astro 7.
- Files check out with LF line endings on every platform.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-05

- Initial commercial release.
- Astro 7 theme for SaaS and product sites with home, pricing, solutions, integrations, customers, about, careers, contact, blog index, topic archive, post, search, RSS, robots, sitemap, and 404 routes.
- Full-width mega menu with featured panels and a full-screen mobile menu.
- Hero with a workflow canvas and a Slack approval over a photo.
- Product UI built in HTML and CSS: workflow canvas, composer, run log, and agent panel.
- Auto-advancing "How it works" tabs.
- Agents, teams, integrations, customer stories, security, and FAQ sections.
- Pricing page with a billing toggle, four plans, and a sticky comparison table.
- Four solution pages from one template.
- Filterable, searchable integrations page.
- Customers, about, careers, and contact pages.
- Markdown and MDX blog with topics, co-authors, a table of contents, a call-to-action panel, a share sheet, and search.
- Photo blog covers and portraits from Pexels with credits.
- Canonical URLs, sitemap, RSS, Open Graph, Twitter/X cards, and JSON-LD.
- Self-hosted Host Grotesk, Lucide icons, and spruce and mint design tokens.
